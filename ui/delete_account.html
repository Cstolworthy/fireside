Delete your account

Last updated: 22 October 2026

You can erase your fireside account and everything in it at any time. There are two ways, depending on whether you still have the app.

From the app

  1. Open fireside and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings, then the Account tab.
  3. Under Delete account, choose Delete my account and confirm.

You confirm with your password. If your account signs in through Google, GitHub, Microsoft or SimpleLogin and has no password, you confirm by typing the phrase delete my account instead.

If you no longer have the app

Email __CONTACT__ from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We verify the request against the account it names, then erase it. You do not need to give a reason.

What gets deleted

  • Your account record and every way of signing in to it (password and any linked Google, GitHub, Microsoft or SimpleLogin identity).
  • Your library: every book you added, the text you imported, and the audio we rendered for you.
  • Your listening history, playback positions, and device sessions.
  • Any speech-provider credentials you stored, and your own shared connections and preferences.

Audio that only your account held leaves our storage, including the copies the shared render cache was keeping so a re-read would be free. A book or recording that another account still holds is not deleted with you; it stays with them.

What can outlive the account

A few things are not ours to erase, and one is worth saying out loud:

  • Billing and tax records. Where tax or accounting rules require it, we keep the record that a payment was made, for the required period.
  • A paid subscription is not cancelled by deleting the app or the account. If you are being billed by card, cancel it from the platform you subscribed through (the billing page) first, or email us and we will stop it. Deleting the account removes your plan here; it does not by itself stop a card charge.
  • Encrypted backups roll off on their own cycle; a deleted account is not restored from them.
